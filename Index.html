Common Questions

What Contractors Ask Before Switching to AI

"Will it sound robotic to my customers?"
No. The AI uses natural voice technology and is trained to sound like a professional receptionist. Most callers cannot tell the difference. We build and test the voice before anything goes live — you approve it first.
"What if it gets something wrong?"
The AI handles over 95% of calls correctly. When it's unsure, it takes the caller's information and flags it for a human follow-up. Nothing falls through the cracks — it always captures the lead.
"Can a human take over if needed?"
Yes. Emergency calls get immediately routed to your on-call technician. For any call where a human is specifically requested, the AI collects their info and ensures a callback within the hour.
"Will this work after hours and on weekends?"
That's exactly when it's most valuable. The AI runs 24/7 with no degradation in quality — same response at 3am Sunday as 10am Tuesday. After-hours calls are often your highest-intent leads.
"Can it integrate with my CRM?"
Yes. We integrate with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, GoHighLevel, and most major CRMs. Every booked job syncs automatically — no duplicate data entry, no manual logging.
"Do I need to change my phone number?"
No. Your existing number stays exactly as it is. We set up a forwarding line — callers dial your number as always, the AI handles it behind the scenes. No disruption to your current setup.
