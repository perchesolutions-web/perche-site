Questions

Everything You Need to Know

How long does setup take?+
Most clients are live within 5 to 7 business days. We handle the entire build — scripting, testing, CRM integration, and call forwarding setup. You review and approve before anything goes live.
Will callers know they are talking to an AI?+
Our AI uses natural voice technology and is trained to sound like a professional receptionist. Many clients tell us callers cannot tell the difference. If a caller directly asks, the AI is honest. We never deceive customers.
What happens if the AI cannot handle a call?+
The AI handles 95% or more of inbound calls. For situations outside its scope, it takes the caller's information and ensures a human follows up promptly. Nothing falls through the cracks.
Do I need to change my existing phone number?+
No. We set up a dedicated AI number that your existing number forwards to. You keep your number, your callers notice nothing different, and the AI handles everything in the background.
Which CRMs do you integrate with?+
We currently integrate with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, GoHighLevel, and most major CRMs via API. If you use something different, contact us — we can usually build a custom integration.
What does fully managed actually mean?+
It means you do not touch anything. We build it, test it, launch it, monitor it weekly, optimize it monthly, and fix anything that breaks. You receive a monthly report showing exactly what the system is doing for your business.
Is there a long-term contract?+
No long-term contracts. Our clients stay because the system works, not because they are locked in. Month-to-month after initial setup. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice.
What is included in the free 30-day trial?+
Everything. We build your complete AI receptionist, integrate it with your systems, and run it live for 30 days at no cost. No setup fee, no monthly charge. At the end of 30 days you decide whether to continue. Most clients see ROI within the first week.
