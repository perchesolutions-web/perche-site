How accurate is a satellite estimate?

Close enough to qualify a lead, not close enough to replace a site visit.

A traced line on satellite imagery can be off from the real yard because of tree cover, grade, setbacks or an outdated photo. That is why TraceQuote shows the price as an estimate and you confirm the final price after measuring on site. The tool is for the first question, not the last one.
