How it works

Questions

What is TraceQuote?

Fence software that adds an instant quote calculator to a contractor’s website. Homeowners trace their fence on a satellite map and see the contractor’s prices in about a minute.

Who sets the prices?

You do. Every number in the tool comes from your price sheet.

How accurate are the quotes?

They are estimates from a satellite measurement. You confirm the final price after measuring on site.

What do I receive when a homeowner asks for a quote?

The address, footage, fence type and height, the price they saw, their name, phone and email, and when they want the fence installed.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Homeowners can trace their fence with a finger on a phone.

What does it cost?

$149 a month with no setup. For our first three fence companies, nothing is paid until your first quote request.

Do I need a new website?

No. It adds to your current site with one embed line, or works as a link.

Can I see it before I decide?

Yes. Try the demo, or book a call and we will show you a preview with your company name on it.
