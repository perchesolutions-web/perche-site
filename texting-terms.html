TraceQuote Privacy policy

Texting terms

TraceQuote quote request alerts. Last updated November 2, 2026.

What you’re signing up for

Fence companies that use TraceQuote can get a text message each time a homeowner sends a quote request on their TraceQuote quote page, and when a homeowner picks a time for an on-site measure. The text has the homeowner’s name, phone number and address, and what they asked for.

You sign up by entering a mobile number and checking the text alerts box on our setup form at perchesolutions.com/setup. The texts come from TraceQuote, a service of Perché AI Solutions LLC.

The details

  • After you sign up, you get one text confirming it.
  • Message frequency varies: one text per quote request or measure time, so it depends on how many requests you get.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop alerts. You’ll get one text confirming it, and no more alerts after that. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Text alerts are optional and not a condition of buying TraceQuote. Every request is emailed to you either way.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your privacy

We use your mobile number only to send these alerts, through our texting provider, Twilio. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. See our privacy policy.
