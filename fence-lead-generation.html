Where fence leads leak

Follow one homeowner from search to your inbox and count the places they can quietly leave.

The slow answerThey wanted a number. You asked them to wait for a callback. They asked three other companies.
The long formEvery extra field loses people. Ask for the minimum, and ask after you have shown them something.
The mobile problemMost homeowners find you on a phone. A form that is hard to thumb through loses them.
The silent follow-upA request that sits unanswered for a day is usually gone. Respond while they are still looking.

What to fix first

In order of effort, from cheapest to most work.

1. Answer fasterSet a rule for how quickly every request gets a call or text. Same day is a good starting point.
2. Shorten the formName, phone, address. Everything else can be asked on the call.
3. Show a price on the siteEven a rough range filters out price shoppers and warms up serious buyers.
4. Give them a way to price their own yardThis is what an instant fence quote does. They trace the fence and see your price without waiting.
