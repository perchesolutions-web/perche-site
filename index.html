Questions

Do homeowners have to give their contact details to see a price?

No. They see prices for every fence type right away. To get the itemized quote or book your free measure, they enter their name, email and mobile number. That’s when the request comes to you.

Will homeowners see my price sheet?

They see a range for their own project, not your sheet. If you’d rather, they can see only a starting price, or no price at all and ask you for it. Every quote says the final price is confirmed after you measure on site.

How is the price worked out?

The traced length, minus gate openings, times your price per foot for the fence type and height they pick. Then your gate prices, old-fence removal if they ask for it, and your minimum job. It’s always a range, rounded to the nearest $50.

How accurate is the satellite measurement?

Close enough for a budget range. Trees, slopes, property lines and permits can change the real job, which is why every quote is a range and your on-site measure sets the final price.

What if the satellite photo doesn’t show the yard?

Homeowners can type their address and a rough length instead. Those requests are marked as typed in, so you know to measure before you quote.

Do I need new software or a new website?

No. We add the quote page to the website you have, or give you a link you can share on Google, Facebook and in texts. Requests come to your email.

How is this different from Angi or HomeAdvisor?

Lead marketplaces often sell the same homeowner’s request to several companies, and you pay for each lead. TraceQuote runs on your own website at one flat monthly price. The request isn’t sold to other fence companies, and it doesn’t cost more when more homeowners send one.

How fast do requests reach me?

By email, the moment the homeowner sends one. The subject line tells you who it is, where the job is and what they picked.

Can I change my prices later?

Yes. Send us the new numbers and we update your page.

Who owns the requests homeowners send?

You do. We store them for you, deliver them to you, and use them only to run and improve TraceQuote. We don’t sell them or pass them to other fence companies. On the founding offer, you agree that we can share your results, such as your company name and how many requests your page received. We never share homeowners’ personal details.

How do I cancel?

Email hello@gettracequote.com any time. Month to month, billing stops at the end of the current month. On a 12-month plan, cancelling in the first 12 months makes the $499 setup due. A yearly plan stops renewing, with no refund of the unused part except under the guarantee. The founding offer has no cancellation fee. When service ends, we take your page down, and you can ask for a copy of your requests within 30 days. The customer agreement has the details.

What does it cost?

The quote page is $149 a month. Month to month, there’s a one-time $499 setup. On a 12-month plan there’s no setup fee, and if you cancel in the first 12 months the $499 setup is due. Or pay $1,490 a year up front, also with no setup fee. With Google Ads management it’s $799 a month plus setup, and your ad budget is billed to you by Google. Our first three fence companies pay no setup fee and nothing until their first quote request comes in, then $149 a month, locked in.

What if no requests come in?

On a paid plan, you get your money back. Once your page is live, with the button on your homepage and the link on your Google Business Profile, if no homeowner sends a quote request within 60 days, we refund everything you’ve paid and you can cancel with nothing more owed. On the founding offer you pay nothing until your first request comes in, so there is nothing to refund.

Do you work with trades besides fencing?

We start with fencing. If you also install pavers, turf or screen enclosures, mention it on the call.
