Pricing math

How a quote is calculated

The actual math, in plain English. You set every number. The prices below are the samples used in the demo.

ItemHow it's countedSample price
Fence lineDrawn length minus gate openings, times your price per foot for the material and height the homeowner picks.Vinyl privacy, 6 ft: $34–$46 per ft
Walk gateFlat price per gate. Its 4 ft opening comes off the fence footage.$450–$750
Drive gateFlat price per gate. Its 12 ft opening comes off the fence footage.$1,400–$2,600
Old fence removalPer foot of drawn line, only when the homeowner asks for it.$2–$4 per ft
Minimum jobWhen a quote comes in under your minimum, the page shows the minimum.$1,500

Always a range. The low end uses your lowest prices and the high end your highest, rounded to the nearest $50.

Never a final bid. Every quote tells the homeowner the final price is confirmed after you measure on site.

Questions

Common questions

What does TraceQuote actually do?

It adds a quote page to your website. Homeowners search their address, trace the fence on satellite imagery, and see a price range for every fence type you install, built from your prices. They can compare heights and colors, see a monthly payment estimate, and book your free measure. You get their contact details, the layout they drew, and the option they picked. We set up text and email follow-up so the lead hears from you right away.

Do homeowners have to give their info to see a price?

No. They see prices for every fence type right away. To get the itemized quote by email or book your free measure, they enter their first and last name, email, and mobile number. That's when you get the lead.

Will homeowners see my exact prices?

They see a range for their own project, not your price sheet. The page also tells them the final price is confirmed after you measure on site.

How accurate is the satellite measurement?

Close enough for a budget range. Trees, slopes, property lines, and permits can change the real job, which is why every quote is a range and your on-site measure sets the final price.

Do I need new software or a new website?

No. We add the quote page to the site you have, or give you a link you can share on Google, Facebook, and in texts. Leads come to you by text and email.

Who sends the follow-up texts?

We write the messages and set them up under your business name. Homeowners choose whether to get texts when they ask for a quote, and they can reply STOP at any time.

What does it cost?

$299 a month plus a one-time $499 setup. Ads management is $799 a month plus your ad budget. Founding pricing is available for our first three Miami fence companies.

Can I change my prices later?

Yes. Send us the new numbers and we update the page.

Do you work with services besides fencing?

We start with fencing. If you also install pavers, turf, or screen enclosures, mention it on the call.
