Doesn't Jobber already do this?

Your field service software can remind you. It treats every quote and invoice the same. Perché works out which ones are worth your time first.

Your field service software's reminders

  • How items are orderedUsually by date or by the order records were created.
  • What a reminder saysThe same wording for every customer, on a schedule you set.
  • What it looks atIts own records, one list at a time.
  • What it tells you todayThat a reminder is due.
  • How it measures successWhether a reminder was sent.

Perché

  • How items are orderedBy dollar value, how cold it is, and the customer's lifetime value.
  • What a message saysWording that fits the situation: a quote, an invoice, how overdue it is.
  • What it looks atQuotes, invoices, past customers, leads and reviews in one ranked list.
  • What it tells you todayWhich $4,200 quote to call first, and why.
  • How it measures successWhether money came back, with identified, expected and recovered kept separate.

Field service software is great at running the job. This describes how reminder tools in that software typically work; exact features vary by product and plan. Perché sits on top of it and doesn’t replace it.

How we decide what to flag

The actual rules, in plain English. These are the defaults every account starts with.

FlagWhen we raise itDefault
Cold estimateAn estimate that was sent and is still open 14 or more days later.14 days
Unpaid invoiceAn invoice that is 14 or more days past due. Customers with a history of paying late are marked lower-likelihood.14 days
Lapsed customerA customer who has had maintenance visits before and has had no visit in 12 months.12 months
Cold leadA new inquiry with no first reply, or one that has had no update for 3 days.3 days
Expiring maintenance planA plan due for renewal within 45 days or already past its renewal date.45 days

You can change the timing. The cold estimate, unpaid invoice and lapsed customer windows are adjustable in Settings, so a shop that closes quotes in three days can set its own clock.

Then we rank. Within each flag, items are ordered by dollar value, how long they have been cold, and what the customer has been worth to you over time. Every flag explains itself in plain English with your real dates and amounts.

No black box. These are plain rules, not a mystery score. If you disagree with a flag, you can see exactly why it was raised.
