Seeing who opened it

Visits from the link are tagged in PostHog with prospect = …. Look for these events: tq_preview_opened, tq_address_picked, tq_quote_viewed, tq_details_submitted, tq_contractor_view_opened and tq_book_clicked. Your own test clicks count too, so open the preview once and note the time.

When they book from their page, the call shows in Calendly with utm_campaign = …. Filter the Meetings page or check the export.

After they sign

Send them the setup form at perchesolutions.com/setup. They enter their prices, lead emails and logo there, and the email you get includes their settings file, ready to build their quote page.
