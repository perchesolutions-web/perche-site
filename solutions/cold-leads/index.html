COLD LEADS

Every inquiry deserves an answer while it's still warm

Perché tracks new leads from your ads, website and software, shows how fast you respond, and flags the ones going cold.

Start 14-day free trialGet in Touch

A homeowner whose AC just died fills out a form at 9 p.m. If nobody replies for two days, they've already hired someone else. The ad dollars spent to get that lead are gone too.

Perché keeps your leads in one list, timestamps your first response, and flags new inquiries that nobody has touched. You also see which sources actually produce jobs.

Bring leads in by uploading a Facebook or Instagram lead-ad export, adding them by hand, syncing Jobber requests, or pushing them through our inbound API from tools like Zapier.

How Perché finds it

Speed and silence, measured honestly.

  • ✓A lead still marked new, or contacted but quiet, for 3 days is flagged (adjustable).
  • ✓Speed-to-lead shows your median response time and the share answered within an hour — timed only from responses made in Perché, never guessed.
  • ✓A source table shows leads, wins, losses and win rate among closed leads for each source, so open leads aren't counted as losses.
  • ✓Leads are kept separate from the main “revenue at risk” total because an inquiry isn't revenue you've earned yet.
