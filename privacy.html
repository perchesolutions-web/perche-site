Privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026.

Perché AI Solutions LLC (“we”) runs perchesolutions.com and TraceQuote, an instant fence quote service for fence companies. This policy covers this website, including the TraceQuote demo.

What we collect

  • How you use the site. Pages you view, steps you reach in the demo, your browser and device type, the site that sent you here, and approximate location from your IP address. We collect this with PostHog, which uses cookies and local storage to recognize returning visitors.
  • Demo links. If we send you a demo made for your company, the company name in the link is used to show your preview. When you use it, we get an email saying it was opened, whether you tried the quote tool, and whether you clicked to book a call, with the company details from the link and whether you were on a phone or a computer.
  • Address searches in the demo. Addresses you type are sent to Mapbox to find them and show satellite imagery. In the demo, the quote request form does not send or store anything you type.
  • Booking a call. When you book through Calendly, Calendly collects what you enter, under its own privacy policy, and shares it with us.
  • Messages. Anything you send us by email or text.

Fence companies’ quote pages

We also host quote pages for fence companies that use TraceQuote, at addresses starting with perchesolutions.com/q/. When a homeowner requests a quote on one of those pages, the request goes to that fence company, and we store it on the company’s behalf. Each of those pages has its own privacy policy and texting terms.

How we use it

  • To run, secure and improve the site and the demo.
  • To follow up with fence companies about demos and calls they booked.
  • To respond to messages you send us.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run the site: Netlify (hosting and form storage), Mapbox (maps and address search), PostHog (site usage), Calendly (call booking), and, for clients, Stripe (payments) and our messaging provider. We don’t sell personal information. Mobile numbers and texting consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or for our business records, then delete it.

Your choices

  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.
  • Reply STOP to any text from us to stop texts.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, email us.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn’t meant for children under 13.

We’ll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
