Side by side

Lifespans are typical ranges. Local climate, install quality and care change them.

Privacy and upkeepTypical lifespan
VinylFull privacy. Wash as needed.20 to 30 years
WoodFull privacy. Stain or seal every 2 to 3 years.15 to 20 years
Chain linkSee-through. Very low upkeep.15 to 20 years
AluminumOpen view, common around pools. Will not rust.20 years or more

How to choose

Want privacy and low upkeepVinyl is usually the match.
Want the lowest price and do not mind upkeepWood or chain link, depending on privacy.
Fencing a poolAluminum keeps the view open and does not rust.
Fencing a large area on a budgetChain link covers the most ground for the least money.
