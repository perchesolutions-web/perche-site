Yes — 14 days on Starter and Professional. No card is required to sign up and see your first dashboard; you add one only when you choose a plan, and you won't be charged until the trial ends.
Starter is the core product: connect your software or a spreadsheet, see flags for cold estimates, unpaid invoices and lapsed customers, work your lead and review inboxes, and follow up by email. Professional adds automatic escalating follow-ups, text messages, Slack alerts, deeper reporting and payment links, plus the First $5K Sprint setup, the text script library, the maintenance plan playbook and the Payback Guarantee.
During your first 90 days on the Professional plan, Perché tracks recovered revenue in your dashboard (the ROI report). Revenue counts as recovered when an estimate that was flagged is later won, or an invoice that was flagged is later paid, after you sent a follow-up from Perché. Wins that happen with no follow-up from Perché are not counted. If that total is less than what you paid us over those 90 days, contact us and we will refund every dollar you paid. The condition is that you send the follow-ups Perché writes for the items it flags. The same numbers you see in the dashboard decide the guarantee.
